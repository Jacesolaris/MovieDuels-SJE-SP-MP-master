Movie Duels is a remastered edition of Wector’s original 2009 mod, Movie Duels 2, for Star Wars Jedi Knight: Jedi Academy. Relive iconic moments from across the Star Wars saga in this full single-player total conversion, featuring new gameplay mechanics and a wide variety of characters and maps.

- Play missions to recreate famous duels across the Star Wars universe.
- New lightsaber combat with manual block, sprint, dash and much more.
- Challenge yourself in the Arena and Challenge modes.
- Huge collection of high-quality characters and maps.
- Modern menus with customizable options such as camera, music and commands.
- Custom animations for characters and cutscenes.
- Expanded force powers and weapons, including repulse, stasis, dual pistols and more.
- Jedi Academy and Jedi Outcast campaign support.
